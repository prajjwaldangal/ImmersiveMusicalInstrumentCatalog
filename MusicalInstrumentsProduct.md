# Building a 3D immersive experience on Quest2 VR with Immersive Web SDK to view musical instruments before purchase. 

## Background
You are a VR expert. A client with a loaded wallet asked to create an e-commerce app on the Meta VR App Store. So, you really wanna do very well and impress the client fully. The client wants his customers to be able to have an immersive experience while browsing the catalog of musical instruments that the client has up for sale. This includes mics, guitars, keyboards, madals, drum sets, etc.

## Tasks
For today you have to create an immersive experience for one of the musical instruments, the mic. It is a diaphragm mic. Create the experience in such a way that it can be easily extended to other instruments in the catalog. In that, the software should be layered and modular. 

## Testing
1. The customer should be able to view all external corners and parts of a given musical instrument.
2. The customer should be able to rotate the instrument 360 degree.
3. The customer should be able to view the instruments clearly by zooming as well as full screen.
